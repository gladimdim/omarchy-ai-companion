Omarchy plugin · Wear OS watch face

Your AI quotas, on your wrist.

A dock widget and a local bridge that read every AI CLI agent on your machine, then drive four quota gauges on a Wear OS watch face. Glance at your wrist to see how much Claude, Grok or Antigravity you have left.

Live on a Galaxy Watch. Four agents, four gauges.

What it does

The laptop does the work: it finds your agents, reads their limits and hands the numbers to the watch. The watch just draws them.

◴

Four quota gauges

Top, right, bottom and left arcs map to four limits you choose. Each one carries its agent's name and percentage around the rim.

⌕

Finds your agents

Claude Code, Antigravity, Grok, Codex and OpenAI are discovered from what is actually installed. Session, weekly and model-level limits all show up as pickable gauges.

⇄

Pairing without typing

Click Add watch on the laptop, tap Connect on the watch. The window lasts 120 seconds and closes the moment one device takes it. A 4-digit code is still there as a fallback.

⚿

Token auth, enforced

The bearer token issued at pairing is checked on every request. Only ping and the pairing handshake are open, so nobody else on the network can read your usage.

⟳

Heals its own address

DHCP moves the laptop and the watch quietly re-sweeps the subnet to find it again. There is no reconnect step, and mDNS still runs as the fast path.

⌂

Nothing leaves the LAN

A zero-dependency Python daemon on the standard library, listening on port 8765. No account, no cloud, no third-party service in the middle.

The widget

It lives in the Omarchy dock as a single icon and opens into everything else: pairing, watch health, and which limit each gauge is tracking.

The watch face

Built in Watch Face Format, so the face itself ships no code. It binds to the four complications on its own the moment you pick it.

Install

Two pieces on the laptop: the dock widget, and the daemon that feeds it.

1

Link the plugin into Omarchy

# from a clone of this repo
ln -s "$(pwd)" ~/.config/omarchy/plugins/gladimdim.omarchy-ai-companion
omarchy restart shell
2

Run the bridge as a user service

mkdir -p ~/.config/systemd/user
cp omarchy-wearos-server.service ~/.config/systemd/user/
systemctl --user daemon-reload
systemctl --user enable --now omarchy-wearos-server.service
3

Check it is up

python3 server.py --status
4

Connect the watch

Click Add watch in the dock widget, then open the Omarchy AI app on the watch and tap Connect. That is the whole setup — nothing is typed on the watch. Then long-press the watch face, Customize, and pick Omarchy AI.

The watch app

The app rides along with the face. It is where the numbers arrive, where you check the link to the laptop, and where you decide which limit each of the four gauges is drawing.

Getting it onto the watch

It needs Wear OS 5 or newer — the face uses Watch Face Format v2 features that older releases do not have.

  • Wear OS 5+, tested on a Galaxy Watch (SM-L705F).
  • Same Wi-Fi as the laptop. A Galaxy Watch parks its Wi-Fi radio while tethered to a phone over Bluetooth; the app says so and offers the Wi-Fi settings screen.
  • No phone app needed. The watch talks to the laptop directly.
  • Prefer to build it yourself? The watch side lives in ai-omarchy-wearos.
